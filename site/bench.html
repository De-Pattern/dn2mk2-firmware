What is exact. STEP, PULS, TRAP and the three wavetables are drawn from the same arithmetic their firmware generators were checked against in an emulator, and a wavetable of yours is drawn through the same reduction and read LFO Waves builds into the firmware — what you see is what the instrument plays. NOIS is the right colour and loop length but not the firmware's exact sequence. The stock shapes marked approximate are the right family; their exact curvature has not been read out of the generator.
