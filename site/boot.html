Digitone II Mods / boot screen

Boot Screen

Your own mark in the Digitone II start-up animation: scattered by the intro's tunnel, held still or flashing, broken into glitching ASCII, or spinning in like a 1960s TV transition. Pick your firmware and a picture, download a signed image.

Everything happens in this tab. Your firmware and your picture are never uploaded — this page has no server to upload them to. It is static files on GitHub Pages; you can read every line in the repository.

Prove your way back before you flash anything. The Early Start-up Menu reflashes stock firmware over MIDI. Do that once, with an unmodified image, before you flash a modified one — and back up your +Drive, which no tool here can recover.

Your firmware

A Digitone II OS 1.11 .syx from Elektron, or one the Modulation Destinations or MIDI Arpeggiator page built. Not one built by LFO Waves: both mods use the same start-up hook.

5

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-09-17. A flashing mark built by this mod booted and played on the instrument. The page and the command line produce the same bytes.

Combines with Modulation Destinations, the MIDI Arpeggiator and the Transient Swapper. Not with LFO Waves yet: both copy data up through the same start-up hook, and this page refuses an image that already carries it.
