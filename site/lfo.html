LFO Waves

Seven new LFO waveforms for the Digitone II, with three wavetables you can keep or replace with your own. Pick your firmware, choose your tables, download a signed image.

Everything happens in this tab. Your firmware and wavetable files are never uploaded — this page is static files on GitHub Pages, and you can read every line in the repository.

Prove your way back before you flash anything. The Early Start-up Menu reflashes stock firmware over MIDI. Do that once, with an unmodified image, before you flash a modified one — and back up your +Drive.

2

The waves

After RAND on every LFO of every track, audio and MIDI. Each draws its own shape on the LFO page, and the SPH knob is renamed for what it does.

3

Your wavetables

POS sweeps through a table: 7 frames, blended smoothly. Keep ours, or replace any of them with your own.

Accepted: a WAV wavetable as Serum, Vital and most wavetable editors export it (frame size read from the file, else 2048 samples; a single-cycle WAV works too), or a JSON table {"frames": [[…], …]} with values from −1 to 1. Any number of frames and any frame length: the table is reduced to 7 frames of 32 points, with levels kept relative to its loudest point.

No wavetable? Draw one from a formula on the LFO Shape Bench, which also shows every wave here across its whole knob range, and download it as JSON.

4

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.
