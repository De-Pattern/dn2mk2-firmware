Digitone II Mods / destinations

Modulation Destinations

Open thirteen more Digitone II parameters to LFO and modulation control. Pick your firmware, download a signed image.

Everything happens in this tab. Your firmware file is never uploaded — this page has no server to upload it to. It is static files on GitHub Pages; you can read every line in the repository.

Prove your way back before you flash anything. The Early Start-up Menu reflashes stock firmware over MIDI. Do that once, with an unmodified image, before you flash a modified one — and back up your +Drive, which no tool here can recover.

What opens

All thirteen, or none. They are found in your image by identity rather than by address, and each is checked to be currently closed before anything is written.

Confirmed on a Digitone II, 2026-09-12. An image opening all 32 parameters the firmware marks closed was flashed. These thirteen appeared as LFO destinations and actually modulated.

Nineteen others stay closed here, deliberately. Chorus and Master parameters were in the same test and none of them appeared — for those the gate is a separate enumeration, not this mask, so flipping it does nothing. Offering you nineteen switches that provably have no effect would be worse than not offering them.

The effects now have their own mod. That separate enumeration was opened on 2026-09-23, so Chorus, Delay and Reverb are reachable after all — through FX Modulation, which combines with this one. Master is still out.

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.
