Digitone II Mods / transients

Transient Swapper

Replace any of the Digitone II's 34 FM DRUM transients with your own samples. Pick your firmware, drop in audio, hear the result, download a signed image.

Everything happens in this tab. Your firmware file and your samples are never uploaded — this page has no server to upload them to. It is static files on GitHub Pages; you can read every line of it in the repository.

Prove your way back before you flash anything. The Early Start-up Menu reflashes stock firmware over MIDI. Do that once, with an unmodified image, before you flash a modified one — and back up your +Drive, which no tool here can recover.

The bank

34 slots. Click a waveform to hear it. Drop audio on a slot to replace it — any format your browser can play, at any rate; it is mixed to mono and resampled for you.

Every slot is exactly 100 ms, and your sample is cut to fit. 4,800 samples at 48 kHz, the same for all 34 — there is no length field to change. Anything longer is truncated; anything shorter is padded with silence at the end. A slot that had to cut your file says so, with the original length, and the start control moves which 100 ms is kept. The slot's waveform shows your whole file with the imported window shaded, so you can see exactly what is kept and what is dropped — and, for a short sample, how much of the slot is silence.

Elektron's own transients do not all fit either: 20 of the 34 are still ringing when their slot ends, and that ring is what you hear at the beginning of the next one. So the factory bank behaves like a continuous 3.4-second recording cut into 100 ms pieces rather than 34 self-contained hits — and replacing a slot quietly changes the opening of the slot after it, because the borrowed tail goes away. A sample that decays inside 100 ms will behave more predictably than the factory content does.

Which slot is which TRAN value is not known yet. The TRAN knob sweeps 0 to 124 across these 34 entries and the mapping has not been measured. Replace a slot, flash, and sweep to find it — and if you work it out, please say so.

Build it

The image is rebuilt, re-signed with the key derived from your own file, and every integrity field is checked again. Nothing downloads unless all of them pass.
