# The Monomachine pages, as the Monomachine draws them (owner, 2026-10-27)

The owner's six screenshots (Monomachine manual): DPRO-WAVE, DPRO-DENS, DPRO-DDRW, SID 6581,
VO-6, and VO-6's V-SW switch. Layout as the Monomachine's main screen: on the left the tempo,
the level bar (LEV), the kit box (KIT:01 / name), the pattern (A01), the machine line
(`DPRO>WAVE`, `SID>6581`, `VO>VO-6`); on the right two rows of four cells, each its name over
its control, dotted separators. An empty place is drawn empty (no knob).

## The controls (owner's descriptions)

- **Knob**: the Monomachine's knob, for every numerical control.
- **DPRO-WAVE WAVE** (and DDRW's WAV1/WAV2's value line, `S01`/`D01`): a box with a bar that
  scrolls through it as the value moves: left to right along a row, top to bottom across the rows.
- **DPRO-DDRW WAV1 / WAV2**: the vertical bar slides along the horizontal line.
- **DPRO-DENS PCH2 / PCH3 / PCH4**: the vertical bar at the bottom of the box moves left or
  right, like a tape deck's VU needle; the value under it (OFF, the ratios, -12..+12).
- **SID's two-position controls** (PWRS, MSRC, ...): a different knob with an indicator in the
  middle that points at one of two positions (off / on), as a knob switch; the value under it.
- **VO-6 CONS**: as DPRO-WAVE's WAVE: the indicator scrolls through the lines, left to right,
  top to bottom, row by row.
- **VO-6 V-SW**: the two-position knob switch (OFF / ON).

## Feel

Choice controls (waves, modes, switches, consonants, PCH's steps): four detents a choice, as
v0.26's sample-machine choices (owner: "perfect, use it for anything down the line"). Numerical
controls: the knob's normal range.

  drawn empty, DDRW's BR1/BR2 bit reduction inaudible.
- SHIFT and GRAN removed (filter types 7, 8).
