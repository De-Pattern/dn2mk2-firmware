# v0.49 · FLEX

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

FLEX, the live-buffer machine that replaces BOUNCE, plus a fairer DSP guard and a steady playhead

**FLEX** (in MACHINE SEL after SLICE)
- recorder trigs on steps record a track, IN or MAIN into one of 8 buffers for R.LEN steps, and the FLEX track plays the take with its own note trigs
- its page is ONESHOT's with BUF 01..08 in SAMP's place: TUNE, PLAY, BUF, STRT, LEN, LOOP, LEV (the fine STRT/LEN/LOOP from v0.48)
- FUNC + VOICE opens FLEX REC, two pages (LEFT/RIGHT):
  - REC TRIGS: [TRIG] places a one-shot (records once, then spent), FUNC + [TRIG] a trig that records every pass. 16 step cells show them: filled a trig, hatched a one-shot, struck a spent one. They're on the [TRIG] LEDs too
  - REC SETUP: SRC, CHAN, BUF, R.LEN, ARM, Q.REC, and the buffer's state (REC, WAIT QREC, READY and its length, EMPTY)
- YES on REC SETUP records by hand, at the next Q.REC boundary when set; NO cancels the wait. ARM OFF keeps the trigs from firing
- a trig while a take runs is refused (BUSY), it never cuts the take
- FUNC + YES saves a take as a sample
- old projects' BOUNCE tracks open as FLEX tracks, recorder trigs kept
- fixed from BOUNCE: a take uses its panel's SRC, CHAN and R.LEN, and takes work on a unit with nothing ever sent to the +Drive

**fixed / new**
- the DSP guard gives sample voices their time first (NiKo): an ONESHOT beside heavy SID/PERC stays clean, and one the guard has to drop stops cleanly until its next note instead of crunching
- STRT/LEN/LOOP turned while a note plays no longer move its playhead (impbox): the note reads on, the new values apply at the next trig or loop pass

**safety**
- new gate checks: FLEX's recorder trigs, panel and LEDs on the ColdFire, and a one-shot trig's take played back by a note with both processors running together, checked from the trig to the sound, with deliberate breaks that must fail
- the guard checked with 12 P-I voices and SID beside an ONESHOT: the ONESHOT sounds exactly as it does alone

**test**
1. MACHINE SEL: FLEX after SLICE; its page shows BUF 01 and EMPTY
2. FUNC + VOICE: FLEX REC; LEFT/RIGHT between REC TRIGS and REC SETUP
3. REC SETUP: SRC IN, R.LEN 16. REC TRIGS: [TRIG 1] (hatched, LED yellow). PLAY with sound into the inputs: one 16-step take, then the cell struck
4. close the panel, a note trig on the FLEX track: it plays the take; STRT/LEN/LOOP trim it
5. FUNC + [TRIG]: records every pass; ARM OFF: it doesn't
6. Q.REC 4, YES while playing: WAIT QREC, then the take at the boundary; NO while waiting cancels
7. SRC TRK n: that track alone; SRC MAIN: the whole mix
8. FUNC + YES: the take saved as a sample
9. two FLEX tracks on BUF 01 and BUF 02: each its own take
10. SID + three PERC + an ONESHOT kick at a high load: the kick stays clean (or stops cleanly, never crunchy)
11. ONESHOT on a long sample, turn STRT while it plays: the playhead keeps going; the next trig starts from the new STRT
12. a v0.47 project with a BOUNCE track loads as FLEX
13. everything from v0.48
