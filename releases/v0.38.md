# v0.38 · JUMP

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

Direct Jump, per-project sample pools, and the fix for silent samples. thanks depaffect, ptkrm, \x0x/ and blskk for the reports.

**fixed**
- **new samples silent until a restart** (since v0.33): samples you add or record now play straight away, recordings even while they're still recording. the sound engine only played slots below a sample count it was told at power-on, and adding a sample never updated that count. this was also why MAIN recordings seemed empty: they recorded fine, but played back silent. a new automatic check adds 22 samples and 3 recordings in a row, and every one has to play
- **THRES 0 started recording on silence**: it now waits for real sound above your input's own noise. THRES 1 and up work like the Digitakt II
- **a crash on SLICE / GRID tracks in KEYBOARD mode** with a chord memory of more than 8 notes

**new**
- **Direct Jump**: hold PTN + YES to turn it on (PTN + NO for off, remembered after power-off). a cued pattern switches at the next step and carries on from where the playing pattern is, like Elektron's Direct Jump. off by default; chains, songs and program changes work as before
- **per-project sample pools**: every project keeps its own samples. NEW starts empty; projects from before this version keep today's pool until you change it. changes are saved right away. while switching projects, a track stays silent until its own sample is loaded, never a wrong one
- the TRIM preview plays while **FUNC + YES are both held**

**test**
1. record a few samples and add a few from +DRIVE: every one plays straight away
2. RECORDER with SRC MAIN while a pattern plays: the recording plays back
3. PTN + YES (DIRECT JUMP ON), then cue a pattern while playing: it switches at the next step and stays in time. PTN + NO: changes at the pattern end as before
4. two projects with different samples: LOAD one, then the other, then back. each has its own pool. NEW: empty pool
5. power off and on: the project you were on comes back with its own pool
6. ARM at THRES 0 with nothing playing: it waits until you play something
7. everything from v0.37 still

**known issues**
- a recording made in a NEW project that was never saved is lost if you load another project without saving, like its patterns. save the project to keep it
- the TRIM preview's playhead doesn't move (the audio plays)
- the start-up animation is a redraw based on s!Rwa²'s design. his own version comes in v0.39
