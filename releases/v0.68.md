# v0.68 · ICONS

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html
manual: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/mod-manual.html

machine icons in MACHINE SEL, and FLEX takes louder

**MACHINE SEL icons**
- move the cursor onto one of the mod's machines and the left pane shows its icon, with its name where SYN was
- PERC, WAVE (DPRO WAVE), DDRW, DENS, VO-6, STAT, PAR, DYN (the three FM+ machines), SID, DT2 (all six sample machines) and FLEX
- the stock machines and MIDI keep the wave and SYN, and the FLTR column is unchanged

**FLEX levels**
- FLEX takes are now 12 dB louder: a take of a track and a take of MAIN or IN both play +24 dB over the old unity
- measured on v0.67 at pattern volume 100, compressor off
- if a take of a loud track sounds crunchy, turn GAIN down: a track's take gets its gain when it is recorded

**please test**
1. FUNC + SRC, scroll the whole list: each mod machine shows its icon and name, the stock ones the wave and SYN
2. LEFT / RIGHT to the FLTR column and back: as before
3. FLEX a single track at GAIN 0: the take sits at about the source's level
4. FLEX REC from MAIN, then from IN: the same
5. a loud source track: does its take clip at GAIN 0?

**known issues**
- the four-letter names sit close to the < > arrows
- a MAIN or IN take saved as a sample plays 24 dB quieter than it did live
- everything listed for v0.67
