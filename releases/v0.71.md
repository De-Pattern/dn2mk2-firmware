FLEX take levels fixed (thanks NiKo)

v0.68 made FLEX takes 12 dB louder. That was the wrong way: NiKo's measurements on v0.67 said they needed to come down. On v0.68..v0.70 takes came in 18-20 dB too hot.

- a take of a track: +6 dB, so with GAIN at 0 it peaks at its source's level
- a take of MAIN or IN: +4 dB
- MAIN and IN takes made on v0.68..v0.70 play at the new level by themselves
- a take of a track made on v0.68..v0.70 stays 18 dB hot: record it again, or set its GAIN to -18

2. the same from MAIN
3. if it's off, tell us by how many dB and from which source
