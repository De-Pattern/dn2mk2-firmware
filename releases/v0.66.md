# v0.66 · NXT

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html
manual: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/mod-manual.html

slides that last until the next trig, FM+ DYN jumps, and FLEX takes of MAIN and IN at a usable level

**slides: NXT**
- a slide now lasts until the track's next trig, like the Analog Four ("the slide is completed when the next trig is reached")
- NXT is SLEN's first choice and what a slide trig gets with no SLEN set
- the next trig can be a note trig or a trigless lock trig, and it wraps round the track's length. no other trig: the whole length
- chained trigless trigs each slide to the next one
- the fixed lengths (1/8 … 64) are still there after NXT, and any SLEN you've set plays as before
- heads up: slide trigs with no SLEN set used to last 1 step. they now slide to the next trig

**FM+ DYN**
- FUNC + turn on 1FRQ and 2FRQ jumps to whole ratios (1FRQ 0, 1, 2; 2FRQ 0 to 4). a plain turn stays smooth, as on the Monomachine

**FLEX: MAIN and IN takes**
- a FLEX take of MAIN or IN was far too quiet, and turning GAIN up clipped it. it's now recorded as it comes in and played back 12 dB up, so it can't clip
- works on FLEX tracks and on any sample machine playing a buffer (SAMP B1–B8)
- takes of a single track are unchanged

**please test**
1. a slide trig with a p-lock and no SLEN, the next trig 4 steps later: the slide takes 4 steps
2. move the next trig: the slide follows it
3. a slide on the last trig: it slides round to the first
4. SLEN set to 1: one step, as before
5. TRIG page 2, hold a trig and turn F: NXT first, then 1/8, 1/4 …
6. FM+ DYN: FUNC + turn on 1FRQ / 2FRQ jumps to whole ratios
7. FLEX REC from MAIN at GAIN 0: the take sits at about the main mix's level, no clipping
8. the same from IN
9. FLEX REC from a track: as on v0.65

**known issues**
- a MAIN or IN take saved as a sample plays 12 dB quieter than it did live
- MAIN may still be a bit short of the source. tell us how it sounds
- micro timing and trig conditions aren't counted for NXT: a trig that won't play still ends the slide
- everything listed for v0.65 except the MAIN / IN level
