# v0.39 · SLIDE

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

Parameter slides, resampling that records the whole take, a +Drive that tells look-alike samples apart, and s!Rwa²'s own start-up animation. thanks paQ, s!Rwa², and everyone who sent +Drive and recorder reports.

**fixed**
- **recordings kept only the first moment, then silence** (MAIN, a track or the input; the length was right, the take was a blip then a flat line, saved that way too): the recorder took its settings from every message the sound engine got, not only its own. v0.38's silent-sample fix sends one at the start of every take, which switched the recording to an empty input about 2 ms in. now only the recorder's own commands (ARM, NOW, STOP, MONITOR) set its source, channel and threshold. a new check records six takes (MAIN, a track, the input; NOW and ARM) and every frame has to match its source from start to end
- **THRES 0 started on hum, hiss or a reverb tail**: it now listens for about 30 ms first, sets its floor from the loudest of that, and follows the background slowly after
- **+DRIVE showed many samples as loaded in one slot** (">A003" on every row, all previewing as the same sound, the others never loading): a slot now remembers exactly which +Drive sample LOAD TO PROJECT put there, not just its name and length

**new**
- **parameter slides**: SLD and SLEN on TRIG page 2. a locked parameter glides from the previous step's value instead of jumping
- **+DRIVE keys like Elektron's**: hold UP/DOWN to scroll (in the menus too), FUNC + UP/DOWN jumps a page, turn the knob while pushed in to scroll fast. NO is still the way out
- **long names**: rows show the start and the end ("KICK_DEE...TH_HIT"), the selected one scrolls to show the whole name
- **ERASE MOD DATA**: for going back to stock for good. at the top of the sample browser, on +DRIVE press RIGHT. it asks twice, erases only the mod's own +Drive areas and checks them after. stock's FORMAT +DRIVE doesn't remove them
- **s!Rwa²'s own start-up animation**, his frames, same timing as before
- **the mod's version on screen**: "MOD 0.39" on the last start-up frame, "MOD v0.39" at the top of the sample browser

**test**
1. RECORDER, SRC MAIN, while a pattern plays: record a few seconds. TRIM shows the whole waveform and the take plays back in full, on a track too
2. the same with SRC on a track, and with the input
3. ARM at THRES 0 with nothing playing (and with a reverb tail dying out): it waits until you play something
4. a pack with look-alike names in +DRIVE: LOAD TO PROJECT one. only that one shows its slot, the others still preview as themselves and load into their own slots
5. +DRIVE: hold DOWN, FUNC + DOWN, turn the knob pushed in. long names show start...end, the selected one scrolls
6. a trig with a locked parameter and SLD on: it glides over SLEN
7. start-up: his animation, "MOD 0.39" at the end; "MOD v0.39" at the top of the sample browser
8. ERASE MOD DATA: NO at either question erases nothing (only try YES YES if you mean it: back up first)
9. everything from v0.38 still

**known issues**
- copy/paste doesn't carry slides yet (pasting a page can drop a track's slides)
- the TRIM preview's playhead doesn't move (the audio plays)
- the +DRIVE preview level wasn't changed: tell us if it's still louder than the same sample on a track
- a recording in a NEW project that was never saved is lost if you load another project without saving
- preset pool trig locks don't work with the mod's machines yet
- ERASE MOD DATA's time on a unit isn't measured yet (tens of seconds to a few minutes)
