# v0.58 · SCALES

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

two microtonal scales in KB SETUP: **Poole Rod12** and **Poole12 Min**

**new**
- KB SETUP (FUNC + KEYBOARD) → SCALE goes on past Locr. bb3 bb7 to **PR12** (Poole Rod12) and **PM12** (Poole12 Min)
- **Poole Rod12** is a 12-note just tuning. it lays out like Chromatic (in a chord MODE it builds the major scale's chords)
- **Poole12 Min** is its 7-note minor (1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7). it lays out like Aeolian
- a track on either plays every note in that tuning, keyboard and sequencer alike, with ROOT as the scale's 1. every other scale is as before

**please test**
1. SCALE: turn past Locr. bb3 bb7: PR12, then PM12, then it stops
2. PR12, ROOT C, against a tuner: C in tune, C# about 47 cents flat, E about 17 cents sharp, B about 51 cents flat
3. PM12: the minor scale's keys; its b3 and b7 sound noticeably flat
4. back to a stock scale: everything in tune as before
5. a sequenced pattern on the track follows the tuning too

**known issues**
- a project saved with PR12 or PM12 shows Locr. bb3 bb7 on stock firmware
