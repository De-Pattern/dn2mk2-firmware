# v0.65 · BANKS

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html
manual: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/mod-manual.html

project RAM in four banks, and FLEX takes of a track at the right level

**PROJECT RAM banks**
- 508 slots: A001–A127, B001–B127, C001–C127, D001–D127, then the FLEX buffers B1–B8
- turn SAMP (or UP / DOWN in the sample list) past A127 and it goes on to B001
- FUNC + UP / DOWN in the sample list jumps a whole bank
- SAMP's box and the list show the bank letter
- LOAD TO PROJECT fills the first empty slot in the bank your track's SAMP is on, then the banks after it
- the +Drive browser's PROJECT RAM shows every bank's samples
- p-locks, sounds and kits keep the bank
- each project keeps its own banks, like bank A
- banks are slots, not more memory: still 400 MB, shared by all four
- every existing project, sound and p-lock is bank A and plays exactly as before

**FLEX level**
- a FLEX take of a single track now plays at its source's level with GAIN at 0. on v0.63 and v0.64 it came out about 12 dB too loud (thanks NiKo for measuring it)
- if you set FLEX tracks to GAIN -12 to fix that, set them back to 0

**more room**
- the firmware is packed tighter (same format Elektron uses): about 87 KB freed for new features

**please test**
1. turn SAMP past 127: the list goes on to B001 and the header shows B
2. FUNC + DOWN in the sample list jumps a bank, FUNC + UP jumps back
3. put a track on a B slot, then +DRIVE > LOAD TO PROJECT: the sample lands in bank B
4. hold a trig and turn SAMP to a C slot: that trig plays the C sample, the others play the track's
5. save the project, load another, load it back: banks B–D are as you left them
6. an LFO on SAMP: it moves through slots but never into another bank
7. an old project: everything in bank A, as before
8. FLEX a single track at GAIN 0: the take plays at the source's level

**known issues**
- the recorder records into bank A only
- a MIDI CC on SAMP picks a slot in the bank SAMP is already in (127 per bank)
- the import page sends into bank A only; fill banks B–D from the +Drive on the unit
- if you kept more than about 31 MB of recordings, banks B–D stay off until the recording at the end of that area is deleted
- FLEX takes of MAIN and IN are still too quiet (fixed in the next build)
- everything listed for v0.64 except the FLEX level
