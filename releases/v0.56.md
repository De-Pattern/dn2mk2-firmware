# v0.56 · DDRW

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

DPRO DDRW's TIME and bit reduction now do something you can hear

**changed**
- **BR1 / BR2**: they followed the Monomachine's own curve, so nothing was audible until about 96. now 0 is still clean, and from the first detent up the bit reduction is heard, getting harsher toward 127
- **TIME**: a wave change (turning WAV1/WAV2, or p-locking them) now glides over real time: about 30 ms at 16, half a second at 64, 2 seconds at 127. 0 is still instant. before, the glide was under 2 ms at any setting
- patcher: test builds can be built in the browser again (thanks NiKo)

**please test**
1. DDRW with two different waves. turn BR1 up from 0: grit from the first detents. same for BR2 (MIX toward WAV2 to hear it)
2. TIME 64, then turn WAV1, or p-lock two different WAV1 values on two trigs: the wave morphs over about half a second
3. older DDRW patches with BR above 0 will sound grittier now: that's expected
