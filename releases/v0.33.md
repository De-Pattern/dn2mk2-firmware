# 🚨 v0.33 is out: STEADY

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

the stability release. thanks to everyone who reported bugs, especially Moog, CAT, Pablo Se7en, Kokozi, blskk and zuzsw.

**fixed / new**
- **all audio cutting out until a restart** (the most reported bug, often after white noise on a PERC track): a Machinedrum or Monomachine voice could run away and poison the delay/reverb. now every sample our voices make is held to a safe level, and a voice that goes bad is wiped on the spot, so it can't reach the effects
- **SLICE / GRID YES menus dead after MACHINE SEL**: fixed (no more pressing SYN first). also fixed: keys that stopped working after some FUNC combos, and the sample list grabbing YES/NO/arrows on another track
- **the DSP** no longer reads samples from outside the sample pool (another way to get silence)
- **LFO DEPTH / SPEED snap with FUNC again** on our tracks
- **+DRIVE**: FUNC + YES held previews a sample (first 10 s, like the DT2). MOVE SELECTED / MOVE HERE and NEW FOLDER in the RIGHT menu
- **PROJECT RAM**: YES ticks samples (YES again unticks), RIGHT acts on them, like +DRIVE and the DT2
- **recorder**: no DEST (it records into the first empty slot, so it can't overwrite a sample). SRC and CHAN are separate (A: IN / MAIN / TRK, B: L+R / L / R). TRIM zoom on F, G moves along
- **FUNC + NO** on the name screen deletes and steps back; hold it to clear
- **slices at NOTE count from C1**, like the DT2 (thanks blskk)
- **SID starts on TRI** when you pick it
- faster +DRIVE browsing with a big library
- the safety checks now include random button-mashing on the sample pages, voices started on garbage memory, 16 heavy voices with every control and effect moving, and an independent code review

**known issues**
- if all audio still cuts out, tell us which machine and what you were turning: the guard stops it spreading, but we still want the cause
- the sample-list freeze and silent samples (Kokozi) haven't been reproduced; videos and steps help a lot
- DPRO DDRW is the heaviest machine: its tables are in slow memory (next)
