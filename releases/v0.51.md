# v0.51 · LIGHTER

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

Lighter Monomachine and Machinedrum machines (more voices before the DSP guard steps in), FLEX settings per track, CLEAR for a buffer, and sample TUNE that plays its fine values

**lighter machines** (they're no longer bit-for-bit copies, but are held to the originals' level and spectrum)
- SID: -64 % DSP (4x oversampling instead of 12x)
- DPRO-DDRW: -43 %, DPRO-DENS: -40 % (read at the output rate, no 2:1 decimator)
- VO-6: -9 % (its formant filters in float)
- Machinedrum TRX hats, cymbal, maracas, cowbell, S2: their filters in float (TRX-CY -33 %, TRX-MA -26 %)
- DPRO-WAVE and the other Machinedrum machines are unchanged
- the DSP guard decides how many voices sound, so lighter machines give more voices at once with no other change

**FLEX**
- SRC, CHAN, R.LEN, GAIN, Q.REC and ARM are now each FLEX track's own (impbox), saved with the kit and following the pattern. A take already recording keeps the settings it started with
- CLEAR: FUNC + NO on REC SETUP asks CLEAR BUF n? for the track's buffer; YES empties it (Error Kit). A take saved as a sample stays
- REC SETUP: the buffer's state (REC, READY, EMPTY, WAIT) and the take's length in the left column, the page arrow below them (it sat under LEVEL)

**fixed**
- sample TUNE plays its fine values (impbox): -0.01 is a hair flat, not a semitone down, and a TUNE LFO glides instead of stepping. All sample machines
- sample TUNE drawn as WaveTone's Osc1 Tune: the octave ruler (a detent 0.01, FUNC + turn 12.00, as before)

**please listen and report**
- SID, DDRW, DENS, VO-6 and the TRX hats/cymbal/maracas against v0.50: still sounding right? DDRW can be slightly brighter (a little aliasing) at very high notes
- how many Monomachine voices you get now before one drops

**test**
1. a SID chord, a DENS chord, a DDRW chord: they sound as before, and more notes play before one drops (FUNC held on the page shows the DSP load, lower than v0.50)
2. two FLEX tracks, different SRC and R.LEN on each: each keeps its own; SAVE, power cycle, reload: kept
3. switch to a pattern whose FLEX track has other settings while a take records: the take finishes as it started
4. REC SETUP, FUNC + NO: CLEAR BUF n?; NO leaves it, YES empties it (EMPTY)
5. a sample at TUNE -0.01 vs 0.00: almost the same pitch; TUNE 0.50: half a semitone up; an LFO on TUNE: smooth
6. the TUNE box: the ruler with octave numbers; a detent 0.01, FUNC + turn 12.00
7. everything from v0.50, including the FLEX level check (record a track at GAIN 0: does it match the track?)
