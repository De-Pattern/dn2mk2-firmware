# v0.48 · FINE

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

the fixes from this week's reports, STRT/LEN/LOOP exactly as the DT2 has them, and BOUNCE's code out

**STRT, LEN, LOOP as on the DT2** (impbox)
- on ONESHOT (STRT, LEN, LOOP) and STRETCH/REPITCH (STRT, LEN) they are now the DT2's fine controls: 0.00..120.00, two decimals, the knob in fine steps with the stock acceleration, push + turn for whole steps. the sound plays from exactly that place (before, only whole numbers reached playback)
- a new ONESHOT/STRETCH/REPITCH starts at LEN 120.00
- older projects are not converted: a LEN saved at 127 plays as 120.00 (the whole sample) and shows 127.00 until it is turned

**fixed / new**
- THRES (Error Kit): threshold recording only started at THRES 0. THRES now follows the meter's scale (its tick is where the take starts), and turning it while armed takes effect at once
- KEYBOARD mode stays on through grid recording: RECORD used to leave it off after a second press
- a turned control's value shows under it for a second on the sample pages, its p-lock's too (impbox)
- SLICE's keys play the slices as the DT2's do: each key its own note, the same slice a trig with that note plays
- the RECORDER's text stays inside its boxes (ON TRK, SAVED, +DRV FULL; R.LEN's MAX on one line)
- BOUNCE's code is out. old projects with a BOUNCE track still load (the track plays nothing, its recorder trigs are kept in the pattern)

**safety**
- new gate checks: STRT/LEN/LOOP's settings swap in and out byte for byte as tracks change, the STRT knob matches the stock fine FREQ knob step for step, KEYBOARD through grid recording, THRES with both processors running together

**test**
1. ONESHOT: turn STRT slowly: 0.00, 0.01, 0.02 ... faster turns jump further; push + turn: whole steps. the start moves by tiny amounts you can hear on a long sample
2. LEN and LOOP the same; LEN at its top stops at 120.00, STRT at 0.00
3. switch to a WaveTone track: its knobs are as before (whole steps); back to the ONESHOT track: STRT still where you left it
4. p-lock STRT on a step to a fraction (e.g. 1.79): that step plays from there
5. RECORDER: THRES 10..30 with a sound over the tick: the take starts; THRES high: armed, turn it down: it starts
6. KEYBOARD on, RECORD on/off twice: KEYBOARD still on
7. SLICE track, KEYBOARD: each key plays the slice a trig with its note would
8. everything from v0.47

**known issues**
- copying a trigless trig doesn't take its SLD/SLEN with it (left as is: the fix wasn't worth the risk)
- an old project's LEN above 120 shows 127.00 until turned
- everything listed for v0.47 except BOUNCE's
