# v0.52 · MUTE

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

FLEX REC can take its source track out of the mix while it still records it, and the RECORDER's R.LEN now counts steps at the pattern's scale

**FLEX: MUTE SRC** (dreamsaremaps, Error Kit)
- REC SETUP's knob H is MUTE (OFF / ON). With SRC set to a track and MUTE ON, that track is out of the mix (its level and its delay/reverb/chorus sends) for as long as MUTE is ON. It still plays, so FLEX keeps recording it
- turn MUTE OFF and the track is back, with a short fade both ways
- it's saved with the FLEX track, like the other REC SETUP settings
- it's not stock mute: the track's LED and the mute screens don't change, and stock mute/solo still work on top of it. "--" shows when SRC is IN or MAIN
- the level meter moved to the left column, under the buffer's state (CLIP shows above it)

**fixed**
- RECORDER R.LEN with a pattern scale other than 1x (impbox's R.LEN 64 not stopping): R.LEN now counts the pattern's steps at its scale. Before, it always counted 1x sixteenths, so at 2x, 64 steps lasted two passes of a 64-step pattern

**please test**
1. a FLEX track, SRC TRK n, MUTE ON: track n goes silent in the mix (sends too) and its LED doesn't change. Record it: the take has the track at full level
2. MUTE OFF: track n comes back. Listen for any click at ON/OFF
3. SAVE, power cycle, reload: MUTE is kept on that FLEX track
4. the RECORDER at scale 2x, R.LEN 16, R.STRT PLAY: the take stops after 16 steps of the pattern

**known issues**
- FLEX REC's PAGE LED doesn't follow the page (NiKo): still open. A photo or video of the page LEDs on stock with a 64-step pattern (page 2 open while it plays page 1) would let us match them
- R.LEN 64 on a FLEX recorder trig: a red trig records every pass by design, so with R.LEN equal to the pattern length it records continuously. Use a one-shot (FUNC + [TRIG], yellow) to record once
