# v0.75 · PILEVEL

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html
manual: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/mod-manual.html

lighter P-I drums, and FLEX takes that follow the track's level

**new**
- the Machinedrum's P-I machines are lighter on the DSP: PI-SD and PI-RS about half their old cost (-47 %), PI-CC, PI-RC and PI-HH -36 %, PI-BD -25 %, PI-MT -23 %. same sound, bit for bit. more P-I voices fit before the DSP guard drops one

**fixed**
- a FLEX take of a track now follows that track's LEVEL. before, FLEX recorded the track before its level, so a quiet track still recorded loud. thanks **NiKo** for tracking it down
- LEVEL 100 records as before (unity with GAIN 0). LEVEL 20 records as quiet as it sounds, LEVEL 127 as loud. takes of MAIN and IN are unchanged

**please test**
1. a track at LEVEL 100, a FLEX take of it, GAIN 0: the take plays at the track's level, as in v0.74
2. the same track at LEVEL 40: the take is as quiet as the track sounds
3. LEVEL 127: the take matches the louder track
4. a FLEX take of MAIN: same as v0.74
5. a kit with several P-I tracks (PI-SD, PI-RS, PI-HH) playing fast rolls or chords: more voices before any drop out, each sounding as before

**known issues**
- this is a 1.11 build. the 1.12 test build (v0.73-t1) is pulled; stay on 1.11 builds for now
- FLEX takes start a little late against their step, about 1/6 of a step (thanks **Dubmaster** for the measurements: 41 ms at 60 BPM, 18 ms at 120). until it's fixed: microtime -4/24 on the FLEX playback trig
- everything listed for v0.72
