# v0.76 · FLOW

FLEX takes on time with their step, and PERFORM macros that move smoothly

**fixed**
- FLEX takes start on their step. before, a take recorded on a step that came round while the pattern played started about 1/6 of a step late (thanks **Dubmaster** for the measurements: 41 ms at 60 BPM, 25 at 90, 18 at 120). now a take recorded on a step and played back on a step lines up with it to within about 2 ms. the microtime -4/24 workaround is no longer needed: set it back to 0
- PERFORM macros glide instead of stepping. a macro's offset is now in the parameter's fine steps and moves there smoothly, so turning a macro no longer zips or jumps (thanks **D4**)
- tip: the filter cutoff is on every track's PERFORM destination list. look for **FREQ**

**please test**
1. FLEX: a recorder trig and a playback trig on the same step (not step 1), pattern playing: the take plays in time with the source, no flam. try 60, 90 and 120 BPM
2. the same with the recorder trig on step 1, started with PLAY: in time as well
3. any FLEX trigs you'd set to microtime -4/24: back to 0, in time
4. PERFORM: a macro on FREQ of a synth track, turned slowly and quickly: smooth, no zipper or steps
5. a macro on several targets at once, PERF MUTE on and off: as in v0.75
