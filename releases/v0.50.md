# v0.50 · LEVEL

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

FLEX takes at the source's level, a GAIN knob, and impbox's fixes

**FLEX**
- a take of a track now comes in at the track's level (v0.49's were very quiet)
- GAIN on REC SETUP (knob G): -24..+38 dB, 0 = the source's level; H shows the source's level meter (CLIP when it clips)
- BUF fixed: BUF on the SYN page and the new C BUF on REC SETUP move the same buffer, the one the track records into and plays (a BUF other than 01 could play the wrong one)

**fixed (impbox)**
- TUNE shows 1.00, not 0.99
- TRK held: the SMP box stays readable
- a p-lock on sample page 2's bottom row shows as locked
- pushing E or F on held steps clears their SLD/SLEN slides

**please test and report**
- REC SETUP: SRC TRK (a playing track), GAIN 0, record, play the take back. Is it as loud as the track? If not, raise GAIN until it matches and post the GAIN number: the emulator can't measure the unit's own level, so this calibrates it

**test**
1. SRC TRK, GAIN 0: take about as loud as the track (report the GAIN that matches if not)
2. GAIN +6 / -6: 6 dB louder / quieter; the meter follows the source
3. SRC IN: as in v0.49
4. C on REC SETUP and BUF on the SYN page follow each other; two FLEX tracks on BUF 02 and 05 each play their own take
5. TUNE 1.00, TRK held, page-2 locks, E/F clearing slides
6. everything from v0.49

**known issues**
- R.LEN with R.STRT PLAY not stopping: not reproduced yet, more details welcome
- R.LEN 64 with a 64-step pattern: looking into it (a red FLEX REC trig records every pass by design)
- FLEX REC's PAGE LED doesn't follow the page (NiKo): open
- STRETCH: an old project that stopped all sound, clicks on short loops: open
