# v0.44 · BOUNCE

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

live sampling redone, the Octatrack's way: a **BOUNCE** machine whose track records into its own buffer on recorder trigs placed with the [TRIG] keys, plays the take in time at any tempo, and saves it to the pool when you want it. v0.41's REC trigs, the two ARM knobs and the REC TRIGS page are gone.

**how**
1. MACHINE SEL → **BOUNCE** (the last sample machine). its BUF knob (D) picks the buffer it records into and plays, 1..8 (two tracks on one BUF: one records, both play)
2. FUNC + VOICE on that track: the **BOUNCE REC** panel. SRC and CHAN (the L/R icon) say what it records
3. **[TRIG 1..16]** places a recorder trig on that step (the 4×4 grid shows the page; PAGE turns it). **FUNC + [TRIG]** places a one-shot: it records once, then shows spent
4. **PLAY**: at the trig the take starts, R.LEN steps at the tempo (MAX = the pattern), into the half of the buffer that isn't playing. when it ends, the track switches to it at the next **Q.PLAY** boundary (PLEN by default, so a loop never plays half-recorded). the panel's status line always says what's happening: EMPTY, n TRIGS: PLAY RECORDS, REC 1.2s, NEXT, PLAYS 2.0s, and why a take was refused (MANUAL REC, NO ROOM)
5. the track plays its buffer with its own note trigs like a STRETCH machine: the take is fitted to its steps, so a loop taken at 120 stays in time at 100. STRT, LEN, PLAY as on STRETCH
6. **YES YES** (twice, quickly) re-arms the spent one-shots. **FUNC + YES** saves the playing take into the next empty pool slot and to the +Drive (SAVING, NORMALIZING, TRIM, FILE NAME, ASSIGN) — the buffer keeps playing it
7. **Q.REC** (E): a manual RECORD NOW (YES) waits for the next boundary while the sequencer plays

**also**
- **CHAN** has its own icon on every RECORDER (L+R, L, R lit), as the DT2 draws it
- FILE NAME: UP/DOWN scroll the character at the cursor (held, they keep going), LEFT/RIGHT move it, FUNC + arrows the character grid, FUNC + NO deletes, FUNC + YES inserts
- recorder trigs fire from the sequencer's own step, with or without a note trig on it, and are saved, copied, pasted and undone with the pattern

**test**
1. new project, T1 BOUNCE, FUNC + VOICE, SRC IN (or MAIN with something playing), [TRIG 1], PLAY: the line reads REC then PLAYS, and T1 plays the loop on its note trigs
2. R.LEN 4 at 120 BPM, then slow the tempo: the loop stays in time
3. FUNC + [TRIG 5]: a one-shot; it records once; YES YES arms it again
4. FUNC + YES: the take lands in the next empty pool slot and in the +Drive's RECORDED
5. two BOUNCE tracks on BUF 1: one with the trig, both play it
6. a plain RECORDER on an ONESHOT track: knob B moves the L/R icon; a manual take as before
7. everything from v0.43 (128 folders, lighter Monomachine machines)

**known issues**
- recorder trigs of a v0.41 pattern (REC 1 / REC 2 locks) show as recorder trigs on a BOUNCE track and are ignored elsewhere
- one recorder: a trig on one buffer while another's take runs cuts that take
- buffers are off in a project with saved samples in SAMP 124-127 (from v0.32 or older)
- the LEDs don't show recorder trigs; the panel's grid does
- an LFO on SAMP or SLCE doesn't step through the loaded samples yet
- preset pool trig locks don't work with the mod's machines yet
- the take's start against the step is unverified on hardware: say if it lands late
- SLICE: with the sample list hovered over empty slots, trigs play the last loaded sample instead of nothing (being fixed for v0.45)
