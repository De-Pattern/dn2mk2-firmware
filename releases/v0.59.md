# v0.59 · FMPLUS

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

the Monomachine's three FM+ machines, and MON on the RECORDER: hear your input while you record it

**new: FM+ STAT, FM+ PAR, FM+ DYN** (MACHINE SEL, after VO-6)
- **FM+ STAT**: two operators. op 1 with its own ratio, fine tune, self feedback (1FB) and a decaying envelope (1ENV); op 2 at its ratio (2VOL above 64 feeds it back). TONE darkens it
- **FM+ PAR**: three operators in parallel, each with its own decay (1ENV/2ENV/3ENV; above 64 it also feeds that operator back)
- **FM+ DYN**: op 1 with a pitch envelope (1FEN) and a volume envelope (1VEN), op 2 with self feedback (2FB) and its envelope
- built from your Monomachine OS file, like the other Monomachine machines: its own sine, ratio, TONE and envelope tables, through its own decimator. checked against the Monomachine's own DSP code on 240 cases. at high self feedback (1FB / 2FB past 32) the Monomachine itself is chaotic, so there the sound matches but not sample for sample
- in tune with the other Monomachine machines at the same note, and each lighter on the DSP than SID

**new: MON on the RECORDER** (knob C, thanks NiKo)
- OFF / ON. with SRC IN and MON ON you hear the input through the main out while the recorder is armed and while it records, at the level it's recorded. "MON" shows in the top right of the record box
- MAIN and TRK sources are already heard, so MON leaves them alone. MON starts OFF at power-on; FLEX REC is unchanged

**v0.58 is not in this build.** its two microtonal scales crashed the unit in a chord MODE, so v0.58 was pulled. this build is v0.57 plus the above. microtuning comes back in v0.60, done a different way (below)

**please test**
1. MACHINE SEL: FM+ Stat, FM+ Par, FM+ Dyn after VO-6. each plays at its defaults, in tune with another Monomachine machine on the same note
2. FM+ STAT: 1FB up to 127 gets noisier and harsher; 1ENV over 64 makes the modulation decay; TONE darkens it
3. FM+ PAR: 1ENV/2ENV/3ENV over 64: each operator decays at its own rate
4. FM+ DYN: 1FEN away from 64 sweeps the pitch at the note's start; 1VEN makes it brighten or fade
5. hold FUNC on an FM+ page: the DSP load
6. RECORDER, SRC IN, something on the input, EXT MIX's input level down: MON OFF, ARM: silent. MON ON: heard while armed and while recording; silent again after the take
7. MON ON with SRC MAIN or a TRK: nothing changes

- MON isn't saved: it's OFF after power-on
