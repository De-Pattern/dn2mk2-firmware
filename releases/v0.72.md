# v0.72 · LIGHTER

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html
manual: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/mod-manual.html

the Machinedrum and Monomachine engines rewritten lighter, part one

the machines spent much of their DSP time reproducing the original chips' exact 24-bit arithmetic, one multiply built out of about 30 instructions. where that doesn't change what you hear, they now use the DSP's own float multiply instead

**lighter**
- EFM-CY 26 % lighter, EFM-CB 12 %, the other EFM machines 7-9 %
- TRX-S2 16 % lighter, TRX-CB, MA, CY, CH, OH 3-7 %
- VO-6 14 % lighter. the DSP limit still allows three VO-6 tracks: four fit on average but dropped a voice now and then in testing, so the limit keeps a margin
- P-I's RC, CC and HH a little lighter
- TRX-CP stays exact on purpose (its clap burst needs it)

**the sound**
- every machine is still checked against the original Machinedrum or Monomachine output: most to within 60 dB sample for sample, the noisy ones (hats, cymbals, feedback FM) by level and spectrum, as since v0.51
- each multiply differs from the original by at most one bit in 24. in the feedback and resonant machines that small difference grows into a different waveform after a few hundred samples, with the same level and spectrum, much as the original noise never repeats exactly

**please test**
1. your Machinedrum and Monomachine sounds from v0.71 projects: do any sound different? especially EFM cymbals and cowbells, TRX-S2, VO-6
2. a busy kit of EFM and TRX machines: any dropped notes?
3. the CPU meter (hold FUNC on a PERC or Monomachine page) on a busy kit: lower than on v0.71?

**known issues**
- the P-I drums (BD, SD, MT, ML, MA, RS) aren't lighter yet: their delay lines need a new memory layout first
- everything listed for v0.71
