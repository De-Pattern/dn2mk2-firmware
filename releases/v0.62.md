# v0.62 · SLICE

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

the slice editor, thanks to impbox's requests (and one from Wolljamin)

**EDIT SLICE POINTS**
- edits play straight away: move START/END, stop turning, hit the slice's key, you hear it. no YES needed (YES and NO still close the editor)
- playing a slice selects it: keyboard, trig keys or the sequencer, the editor follows what plays
- finer zoom: ZOOM goes down to 128 frames, small enough to see single waveform cycles
- zero crossings: FUNC + E (START) or FUNC + H (END) snaps the point to the nearest zero crossing. no more clicks at slice edges

**CREATE SLICE GRID**
- FUNC + E jumps SLICES 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 / 64, one detent a jump. E alone still moves one (thanks Wolljamin)

**please test**
1. move a slice's START, stop turning, play its key: you hear the change without YES
2. play the track: the editor selects the slice that plays
3. ZOOM all the way in: single cycles visible
4. FUNC + E / FUNC + H land on zero crossings; without FUNC they move as before

**known issues**
