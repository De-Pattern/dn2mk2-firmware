# v0.53 · PAGES

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

FLEX polish: the page LEDs follow FLEX REC's page, a new FLEX track plays its whole take, BUF behaves like a choice and its locks play, PLAY turns quicker, R.LEN snaps to bars

**fixed**
- FLEX REC's PAGE LEDs (NiKo): the green page LED stayed where stock had it while PAGE turned FLEX REC's page. Now, while FLEX REC is open, the eight page LEDs show its page the way stock does in grid recording: the page you're on bright green, the pattern's other pages dim red, pages past the pattern's length off. Close FLEX REC and they're stock's again
- BUF on the SYN page: four detents a buffer, and the knob stays on its buffer until the next one, instead of creeping through the detents in between
- BUF p-locked: with the trig held, the highlighted BUF shows its buffer number (01..08), not just BUF
- BUF locks now play (NiKo): a trig with BUF locked plays that buffer. Before, every note played the track's BUF, the same buffer FLEX REC records into, so you couldn't record into one buffer while playing another. Now the track's BUF is where FLEX REC records and what unlocked trigs play; lock BUF on a trig to play any other buffer

**changed**
- switching a track to FLEX sets its trig LEN (TRIG page) to INF, so a playback trig plays the whole take. Only on the switch: saved projects, existing FLEX tracks and LEN locks are left as they are
- PLAY (FWD / FWD loop / REV / REV loop) on the sample machines: two detents a mode instead of four, so all four take six detents
- FLEX REC's R.LEN: FUNC + turn moves a bar (16 steps) a detent and lands on whole bars (MAX, 16, 32 ... 128). Without FUNC, a step a detent as before

**known issue**
- turning PLAY or BUF while holding a trig (a p-lock) still moves through the raw range, many detents a choice, and a held PLAY cell can show a number instead of the mode. Being looked at for the next build

**please test**
1. a 64-step pattern, FLEX REC open (FUNC + VOICE on a FLEX track): page 1 is green. PAGE: the green moves to 2, 3, 4, then back to 1, with the screen's 1/4..4/4. Close FLEX REC: the page LEDs are stock's again
2. switch a track to FLEX in MACHINE SEL: TRIG page LEN reads INF. Record a take and place a note: it plays to the end of the take
3. turn BUF on the SYN page slowly: the knob jumps buffer to buffer, four detents each, no movement in between
4. GRID RECORD, hold a trig and lock BUF to another buffer: the highlighted cell shows the buffer's number
5. record a take into buffer 1, then one into buffer 2 (track BUF 2). Lock a trig to BUF 1: that step plays the first take, the unlocked trigs the second
6. PLAY on a ONESHOT track: two detents to each next mode
7. FLEX REC, R.LEN at 20: FUNC + turn right gives 32, left 16, left again MAX
