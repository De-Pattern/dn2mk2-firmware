# v0.40 · NEST

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

Folders inside folders on the +Drive, a SLICE editor that reads its sample every time, a recorder that normalises like the DT2, slides that survive copy and paste, and a long list of your reports fixed. thanks takton, ptkrm, paQ, Pablo, mattv0216 and everyone who sent reports.

**fixed**
- **SLICE said CANNOT READ IT** on a sample it could play: a time limit was compared the wrong way round. the menu now waits and retries
- **SAMP / SLCE p-locks played the wrong sample or slice** (relative to the track's own): a lock now plays exactly the sample and slice you locked
- **copy and paste dropped slides**: SLD and SLEN now travel with copy, paste and undo (trig, page, track), and clearing a track frees them
- **the TRIM preview's playhead didn't move**: it now follows the audio and goes away when you let go
- **the playhead lagged behind on sample and SLICE pages**: it follows the newest note at once
- **ERASE MOD DATA stopped with TRIM ERROR on some cards**: it now waits for the card, retries, shows a percentage, can pause and resume
- **the +DRIVE cursor moved by itself**: the browser's knob ignores the tiny jitter some encoders send
- **PLAY / STOP / RECORD did nothing while the SLICE menu was waiting**: they reach the sequencer again
- **long values on SYN held could be cut short**: the text has room now

**new**
- **folders inside folders in +DRIVE**, six levels deep, in the browser and in the web import page (and the web page's erase button)
- **recordings normalised to full scale**, as on the DT2 and Octatrack: quiet takes come up to full level, never clipped
- **SLICE start/end LINKED or UNLINKED**, as on the DT2 (knob A, LINKED by default)
- **RECORDER SRC and CHAN**: four detents a choice, so picking a source is easy
- **DIRECT JUMP**: every cue switches at the next step, even fired fast

**test**
1. +DRIVE: make a folder inside a folder (web import page), browse into it on the DN2, LOAD TO PROJECT from it, NO back out level by level
2. SLICE a sample in the menu right after loading it: no CANNOT READ IT; LINKED moves start and end together, UNLINKED moves each on its own
3. lock SAMP (or SLCE) on a trig: that exact sample (slice) plays
4. a track with slides: copy a trig, a page, the track, paste it elsewhere: the slides come along; clear the track: they go
5. RECORDER: record something quiet: TRIM shows it at full level, not clipped; the TRIM playhead moves while you preview
6. RECORDER: SRC and CHAN step one choice per four detents
7. DIRECT JUMP on: tap pattern changes quickly, each switches on the next step
8. ERASE MOD DATA (only with a backup!): it shows a percentage and finishes, or pauses and resumes
9. everything from v0.39 still

**known issues**
- an LFO on SAMP or SLCE doesn't step through the loaded samples yet
- a looping voice's playhead can jump back early on screen (sound is fine)
- folders imported flat by older versions stay flat: delete and re-import them to nest them
- the +Drive library holds up to 2,048 samples and 64 folders
- DDRW at high bit-reduction (around 112) can round one step differently from a real Monomachine (since the Monomachine engines arrived)
- a recording in a NEW project that was never saved is lost if you load another project without saving
- preset pool trig locks don't work with the mod's machines yet
- the +DRIVE preview level wasn't changed

**coming next (not available yet)**
- rec trigs: recording started and stopped by the sequencer, with a set length (Octatrack style)
- parameter slides on the sample, Machinedrum and Monomachine machines' own controls
- a bigger +Drive library
- a closer look at Overbridge with the mod (it works with Overbridge 2.26.10 or later)
