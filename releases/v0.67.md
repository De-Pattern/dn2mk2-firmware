the arpeggiator on MIDI tracks

**arp on MIDI tracks**
- the ARPEGGIATOR menu now opens on a MIDI track
- with the arp on, a MIDI track's notes go through the arp and out over MIDI, one note per arp step, its length the arp's N.LEN (INF is sent as the longest finite length)
- sequencer trigs and the keys both go through the arp
- arp off on a MIDI track: stock MIDI, as before. synth tracks are unchanged
- this is Angel Linares's work from the upstream project this firmware is built on (dn2_firmware_explore), tested on his unit. thanks Angel. it's now part of this build

**please test**
1. a MIDI track to an external synth, arp on, hold a key: the synth plays the arp
2. the same from sequencer trigs
3. change SPD, RNG, offsets, N.LEN: the notes follow
4. stop mid-arp, and turn the arp off mid-note: no stuck notes
5. arp off: the MIDI track sends as before
6. the MIDI track with the arp on makes no sound on the DN2's own outs
7. HOLD on a MIDI track with the arp on (not tested yet)
