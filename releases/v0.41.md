# v0.41 · LOOP

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

Octatrack-style rec trigs with two looping buffers, QREC and QPLAY, slides on our own machines' controls, and fixes from your reports. thanks depaffect, paQ, Pablo and everyone testing.

**new**
- **REC trigs**: FUNC+VOICE (the RECORDER), hold a step, turn H: REC 1 or REC 2. that step records the RECORDER's SRC (IN, MAIN, any track) for R.LEN steps (MAX: the pattern's length). any track, trigless locks too
- **arm them per track**: RIGHT for the REC TRIGS page, H: OFF (default: nothing records), ONCE (records the next pass, then keeps looping it, like the Octatrack's one-shot) or ON (every pass)
- **two buffers, REC 1 and REC 2** (SAMP 124, 125): play them on any sample track. kept in RAM like the Octatrack's, gone on project change or power off
- **QPLAY**: a new take is heard from the next boundary after it's finished, so a loop never plays half a take (OFF: hear it as it records)
- **QREC**: RECORD NOW waits for the next boundary while the sequencer plays (1 to 64 steps, or the pattern's start)
- the **REC TRIGS** page (RIGHT on the RECORDER, now 1/2 and 2/2): both buffers, the track's REC trigs on a 4x4 grid, Q.REC, Q.PLAY, ARM
- **slides on our machines**: TUNE, LEV, STRT, LEN, LOOP on the sample machines, the Machinedrum machines' controls their sound responds to mid-note, and PERC's track FX
- **SLEN**: four detents a choice, like the other choice knobs

**fixed**
- **NORMALIZING sat for minutes**, then went back to the recorder (the take was fine): the screen gave up on a save that was still moving
- **the SLICE menu opened over a project save** on SLICE/GRID tracks: YES while SAVING was on screen went to the menu
- a long recording into a crowded recording area could run into the next recording until a restart

**test**
1. a pattern playing, SRC MAIN: RECORDER, hold step 1, H to REC 1, R.LEN 16; RIGHT, H to ONCE. another track on ONESHOT, SAMP REC 1, a trig on step 1: after a bar you hear the loop, and it keeps that take
2. QPLAY OFF: you hear it as it records. QPLAY PLEN: it switches cleanly at the pattern's start
3. QREC 16, YES while playing: WAITING, then it starts on the bar
4. a sample track, a trig with SLIDE and a TUNE lock: the pitch glides
5. a SLICE track, FUNC+SETTINGS, YES, press YES again while SAVING shows: no slice menu
6. record something long, then FUNC+YES straight away: NORMALIZING finishes, TRIM shows
7. everything from v0.40 still

**known issues**
- one recorder: a REC trig while the other buffer records cuts that take
- buffers are off in a project with saved samples in SAMP 124-127 (from v0.32 or older): they keep playing those
- DDRW at heavy bit reduction rounds a step differently from a real Monomachine (fixed in v0.42)
- an LFO on SAMP or SLCE doesn't step through the loaded samples yet
- preset pool trig locks don't work with the mod's machines yet
