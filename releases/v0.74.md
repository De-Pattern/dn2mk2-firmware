live recording no longer drags the sequencer

**fixed**
- live recording (REC + PLAY) while playing several trig keys at once (6 or 7) made the sequencer lag. thanks **oksami** for the report and the steps
- the cause: each key recorded cleared its step's slide locks in three passes over every lock record. now it's one pass, checking the track first. the result is the same
- measured on the same live-recording run: stock 807k instructions, v0.72 1.11M, v0.74 842k, close to stock

**please test**
1. REC + PLAY with the sequencer stopped, entering notes step by step with 6-7 keys at once: does it keep up?
2. live recording with the sequencer running, chords of 4-7 notes: any lag?
3. slides on a track, then a live recording over some of its steps: the steps you recorded over lose their slide as before, the others keep theirs
