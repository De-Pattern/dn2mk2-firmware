# v0.35 · SWEEP

patcher: de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html

a release of fixes only: the bugs you reported, worked through one by one. thanks flupperich6l, misterairplane, Pablo Se7en, Kokozi, blskk and everyone who posted reports.

**fixed**
- **MACHINE SEL: the filter list showed up empty** after choosing one of the new machines and pressing RIGHT. our machine list is long enough to scroll and the filter list kept that scroll position. it now opens on your filter. the safety checks now look at what the screen actually draws there, so it can't come back unnoticed
- **FUNC + YES preview in +DRIVE kept restarting** while held: it plays through now
- **holding SYN shows the values** on our machines' pages, like the stock machines
- **LOAD TO PROJ sometimes didn't load a sample** (samples of the same length with similar names): fixed
- **deleted folders stayed in the sample manager list**: gone now. the import page's "Delete selected" can remove folders too (needs this build)
- **another PERC track's names on the LFO page** when switching tracks: fixed
- if a sample previews but won't play on a track: `python tools/dn2diag.py entries` says why

**test**
1. MACHINE SEL, pick GRID or SID, YES, RIGHT: the filters are listed (from MULTI-MODE)
2. +DRIVE: hold FUNC + YES on a sample: it plays through
3. hold SYN on a PERC, Monomachine and sample page: values show; let go: labels
4. LOAD TO PROJ a handful of samples from one pack: each lands in its own slot
5. tick a folder in +DRIVE and DELETE it: it's gone from the list
6. still from v0.34: P-I BD locked to 1 voice sounds every note. if any voice (P-I, VO-6) goes silent, hold FUNC right away and tell us what the tempo's place says (`V1D` / `V1X`)

**known issues**
- why a P-I or VO-6 voice sometimes goes bad on the instrument: still being tracked down (the `V1D`/`V1X` readout is how)
- "only two Monomachine voices" (one report): not reproduced, need details: which machines, firmware, a fresh project?
- arpeggiator / note edit crash: need the exact steps

**coming next (not available yet)**
- the cause of the bad P-I / VO-6 voices, from your readings
- moving the Monomachine tables to fast memory (lighter DDRW / DENS)
- rename and move from the import page
- a MACRO page: knobs that each turn several mappings at once, live (A4-style perf macros)
